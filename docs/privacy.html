ASTRA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

ASTRA is a privately operated video creation and YouTube publishing workflow. Its public information pages do not request visitors' Google passwords or OAuth tokens.

When the channel owner authorizes YouTube access, ASTRA uses Google OAuth credentials to check the authorized channel and publish videos on that owner's behalf. OAuth credentials are intended to be stored as protected GitHub Actions secrets rather than in public repository files. Video identifiers and workflow outcomes may be retained in operational logs and state files to prevent duplicate publication and troubleshoot failures.

Google's YouTube services are subject to the Google Privacy Policy. The channel owner can revoke ASTRA's access from their Google Account connections.

This notice describes the intended ASTRA workflow. Operational behavior should be reviewed before treating it as a compliance certification.
