# ASTRA Content Portfolio & Diversity System

## Objective
Build a narration + synchronized-captions Shorts channel that feels like a varied discovery feed, not one template repeated across unrelated subjects. No animation, B-roll, stock footage, or visual-scene instructions.

## Editorial pillars
1. **Solve it** — logic puzzles, riddles, visual-free memory challenges, estimation, paradoxes.
2. **Know something surprising** — science, space, nature, human body, geography, history, inventions.
3. **India decoded** — Indian history, geography, civics, culture, infrastructure, overlooked stories.
4. **World decoded** — countries, borders, languages, institutions, unusual customs, global GK.
5. **Human behaviour** — psychology, decision traps, social behaviour, perception, everyday biases. Evidence-aware; no diagnosis.
6. **Money & power** — business models, economics, consumer tricks, company case studies, money concepts. No unsupported financial promises.
7. **What's happening** — current news and trends explained with dated, attributable source packs. Never manufacture urgency or report unsupported claims.
8. **Imagine the consequence** — grounded what-if scenarios, counterfactuals, science thought experiments; label fiction/speculation.
9. **Original mini-stories** — mystery, thriller, sci-fi, emotional dilemma, twist, moral choice. Original characters and plots only.

## Creative treatment rotation
Rotate the *viewer experience*, not just the topic:
- Cold-open mystery
- Make-a-guess / answer reveal
- Two choices, then consequence
- Myth vs evidence
- Timeline reversal
- One object, hidden history
- A number that changes the story
- Case study: decision → consequence → lesson
- Prediction before explanation
- Three clues, one reveal
- Counterintuitive claim → proof → limitation
- What changed / why it matters / what's uncertain

Do not use the same treatment in consecutive uploads. Do not make every Short a question-and-answer. A viewer question is optional when it feels forced; if retained for pipeline compatibility, it must be specific and natural.

## Diversity controls for every batch
Each candidate should carry metadata for:
- pillar and subtopic
- treatment / engagement mechanic
- factual, sourced-current, or fictional evidence class
- emotional tone (curious, tense, playful, awe, thoughtful, urgent-but-calm)
- opening pattern
- payoff type
- named entities / key concepts

Batch selection should enforce:
- no duplicate pillar in adjacent slots where alternatives exist
- no repeated treatment in adjacent uploads
- no same opening pattern more than twice in the last five
- no repeated payoff type more than twice in the last five
- no near-duplicate topic/entity cluster in recent history
- a mix of evergreen and timely topics; news never dominates the feed
- a minimum of three distinct pillars in every four-video batch

## Editorial quality rules
- Facts: support specific claims with reliable evidence; avoid invented numbers and false precision.
- News/trends: use at least two independent, relevant sources; preserve publisher and publication date; if evidence is thin, skip the item.
- Puzzles: exactly one defensible answer; all conditions stated before reveal.
- Psychology/health: distinguish evidence from speculation; do not diagnose or prescribe.
- Money/business: explain mechanisms and trade-offs, not get-rich claims.
- Fiction/what-if: label speculative elements and do not present fiction as fact.
- Titles and openings must be specific to the actual payoff, not generic clickbait.
- Narration must sound natural aloud; captions must remain readable and synchronized.

## Suggested four-upload pilot mix
1. Solve it — short puzzle with a timed thinking pause.
2. Know something surprising — science or nature with a counterintuitive reveal.
3. India / World decoded — a lesser-known fact with context.
4. Money, behaviour, or a sourced trend — rotate this slot weekly.

This is an editorial direction, not a performance guarantee. Evaluate the first 20–30 published Shorts using viewed-vs-swiped, average view duration, average percentage viewed, opening retention, rewatches, shares, comments, and subscribers gained. Publishing remains disabled until separately approved.
